# CalorieKnight

A medieval RPG where logging food is how you fight. One Flutter codebase for Android, iOS and the web, built on the same food engine as The Calorie Card.

## First run

This folder holds the app code (`lib/`), tests and config. The platform folders (`android/`, `ios/`, `web/`) are generated by Flutter, so create them once:

```
cd C:\CalorieKnight
fvm use <the same Flutter version as TheCalorieCard>    # or skip if you use a global flutter
flutter create --org com.hayersoftware --project-name calorieknight --platforms android,ios,web .
flutter pub get
flutter test
flutter run -d chrome          # or an Android device/emulator
```

`flutter create .` leaves the existing `lib/`, `test/`, `pubspec.yaml` and `analysis_options.yaml` alone and only adds what's missing.

Needs Flutter 3.27 or newer.

## Signing in (testing build)

The app opens on a sign-in screen. Accounts are mocked on the device until Firebase is connected:

- **Demo account:** `demo@calorieknight.test` / `Excalibur-2026` (there's also a "Fill in demo account" button)
- **Create an account** works too; each account gets its own hero, save and friends.
- **Sign out** is on the Profile tab.

## Screens

1. **Sign in / Create an account**
2. **Choose your path** (once per hero): knight, mage or ranger.
3. **The app**, with HP, XP and level fixed at the top and a bottom bar like The Calorie Card's (a sidebar on tablets and desktop):
   - **Camp**: the current foe, **Rest at the Inn**, the merchant, today's rations and the battle log.
   - **Hero**: your painted hero, equipped gear (tap a piece for its stats), totals, satchel, and **Retrain at the Inn**.
   - **Strike** (the raised round button in the middle): log a meal and watch the attack.
   - **Friends**: add friends, send cheers, the weekly **Hiscores** and the co-op **Raid**.
   - **Profile**: account, username and invite link, settings, sign out.
4. **Battle**: opens when you log a meal, or after the Inn when the morning brings a finishing blow. Knights swing, mages cast a bolt, rangers loose an arrow. After every victory come the **spoils** (equip or sell), then the **merchant**.

## Classes

| | Knight | Mage | Ranger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trait | +15 max HP, 5% less damage taken | +3 fire on every full swing | 15% critical hits (double damage) |
| Armour (merchant) | Padded › Chainmail › Plate › Gilded | Apprentice › Arcane › Archmage › Astral robes | Woodsman › Studded › Brigandine › Shadowweave |
| Weapons (loot) | swords, falchion, axes, mace, morning star, warhammer | staves, crystal staff, wands, sceptre | bows, longbow, crossbows |
| Off-hand (loot) | buckler, kite and tower shields | tome, orb | quiver, parrying dagger |
| Head (loot) | nasal helm, great helm, sallet | pointed hat, cowl, circlet | scout's cap, ranger's hood, mask |

Armour tiers reduce damage by 0 / 15 / 30 / 40% and cost 0 / 120 / 300 / 600 gold. Retraining costs 100 + 20 per level; you keep level and gold, and each class keeps its own armour and gear.

## Loot

Every defeated foe drops one piece of gear for your class (trinkets suit any class). There is no inventory: **equip** it (the piece it replaces is sold) or **sell** it. A drop you haven't decided on waits at camp.

- **Rarity:** common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary (bosses always drop rare or better). Higher rarity means bigger numbers and more enchantments.
- **Effects:** damage, poison, fire, frost, holy (against the undead), bleed (overnight), critical chance, life steal, guard, max HP, regeneration at the Inn, thorns, fortune (gold), wisdom (XP) and dragon fire.
- **Named legendaries:** Oathkeeper, Dawnbreaker, Grimtooth, The Warden's Bulwark, Crown of the Fallen Rose, Emberheart, Rimefrost Sceptre, The Verdant Wand, Tome of Endless Pages, Circlet of Quiet Stars, Thornwhisper, Galeforce, Stinger, Quiver of the Hollow Moon, Mask of the Silent Wood, Amulet of the Hearth, Gambler's Coin, Wyrm's Eye Pendant, Locket of Second Wind and Ring of the Greywood.
- Weapons glow with their element (poison green, fire orange, frost blue, holy gold); legendary gear sparkles.

## Friends

The friends list is mocked on the device with a few fictional heroes until Firestore is connected. It mirrors The Calorie Card's shapes (`users/{uid}.friends`, `friend_requests`, `usernames/{handle}`, and invite links like `calorieknight.com/?add=handle`).

- **Add** by username or email, or share your invite link. In the prototype, try `wren`, `isolde`, `gareth` or `cedric`; they accept after a few seconds. One request (Rowan) is waiting when you first open Friends.
- **View hero**: a friend's painted hero, gear and week. Friends never see meals or calories.
- **Cheer**: once a day per friend. Each cheer you receive adds 5% damage until you rest (up to three).
- **Hiscores**: ranked by balanced days this week, then streak. Never by calories.
- **Co-op raid**: one boss per week for the whole party. Every balanced or lean day at the Inn also strikes it for twice your finishing blow. Fell it and everyone who helped claims +120 XP, +150 gold and a rare-or-better drop.

## How it's organised

| Folder | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| `lib/game/` | The rules, in plain Dart so they can move to a server unchanged: `catalog.dart` (foods, foes, prices), `classes.dart` (classes, armour tiers, retraining), `loot.dart` (rarities, stats, bases, affixes, legendaries, the loot table), `models.dart` (save data; old saves migrate), `rules.dart` (formulas), `engine.dart` (every player action). `controller.dart` connects the engine to the screens. |
| `lib/art/` | Every character is drawn in code on one jointed skeleton (`rig.dart`). `looks.dart` dresses it (`heroLook` builds any class from its loadout), `body.dart` and `heads.dart` paint bodies, robes, quivers, shields, helms, hats and every weapon, `gear_icons.dart` paints loot icons with the same art, `poses.dart` holds stances and the swing, cast and shoot animations, and `scenery.dart` paints the realms and the Inn. |
| `lib/ui/` | `shell.dart` (bottom bar, Strike orb, sidebar), `camp_page.dart`, `hero_page.dart`, `friends_page.dart`, `profile_page.dart`, `class_picker.dart`, `loot_window.dart`, `merchant.dart`, `battle_screen.dart` and `battle_stage.dart`, `hero_view.dart` (painted heroes and gear tiles), `hud.dart`, `journal_panel.dart`, `auth_screens.dart`, and `windows.dart` (log food, the Inn and verdict, settings). |
| `lib/services/` | `auth.dart` (sign-in interface and mock), `friends.dart` (friends, cheers, hiscores and raids: interface and mock), `storage.dart` (one save per account), `food_search.dart` (where the real food engine plugs in). |
| `test/` | Rules tests: one balanced day per common foe for every class, bosses take about three days, undereating never pays, the squire check, falling, loot (equip, sell, boss rarity), retraining, cheers, raids, old-save migration and save round-trips. |
| `tool/simulate.dart` | `dart run tool/simulate.dart` plays 20 balanced days and prints when each foe falls. |

## Connecting the real food engine

The prototype menu comes from `SampleFoodSearch`. To use the fatsecret-proxy, pass a `ProxyFoodSearch` where the controller is made (`_controllerFor` in `main.dart`):

```dart
_game = GameController(
  storage: GameStorage(accountId: a.id),
  foodSearch: ProxyFoodSearch('https://<your-proxy>.onrender.com'),
)..load();
```

Then match `ProxyFoodSearch.search` in `lib/services/food_search.dart` to the proxy's real endpoint and response shape. The easiest path is to copy The Calorie Card's existing client into it. `tagFromCategory` turns FatSecret categories into the ale, fast-food and sweets tags that drive the status effects.

## Connecting Firebase later

- **Auth:** write a `FirebaseAuthService` that implements `AuthService` in `lib/services/auth.dart` (four methods: `restore`, `signIn`, `signUp`, `signOut`) and use it in `main.dart` instead of `MockAuthService`.
- **Saves:** give `GameStorage` a Firestore version keyed by the account id (one document per player holding `GameState.toJson()`), then move the rules in `lib/game/engine.dart` server-side so gold and gear can't be edited on the phone. Loot rolls should move server-side too.
- **Friends:** write a `FirestoreFriendsService` that implements `FriendsService` in `lib/services/friends.dart` and pass it in `_controllerFor` (`main.dart`) instead of `MockFriendsService`. The file header lists the collections. Raids need a small Cloud Function (or a transaction) so blows from several friends add up safely.
